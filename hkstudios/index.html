AI Content Platform · Seoul

From a one-line brief to finished, on-brand content.

HK Studios (에이치케이스튜디오) builds an AI content platform that turns a short input, such as a product photo, a birth date or a market ticker, into finished images, short-form video, carousels and long-form personalized reports, then publishes them across social channels and mini-apps.

01 · Brief

Structured order form or API input per brand.

02 · Compute

Deterministic engines (calendar math, quant indicators) produce the facts.

03 · Generate

LLMs write; image and video models render in the brand's style.

04 · Publish

Human QA, then automated publishing via platform APIs.

One pipeline, many content formats

The same engine serves multiple brands, each with its own voice, templates and credentials.

AI product visuals

Product, texture and model shots plus ad creatives in Meta and GFA sizes, generated without a photo shoot.

Short-form video

One product photo becomes a 1080p vertical video ad, with captions and logo added in editing.

Personalized long-form reports

A deterministic calculation core plus LLM interpretation renders multi-page PDF reports for each customer.
